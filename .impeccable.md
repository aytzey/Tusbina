## Design Context

### Users
Turkish medical students preparing for the TUS exam. They use the app while commuting, studying late at night, and in short sessions between classes. They're under pressure, time-poor, and need an interface that respects their focus — no friction, no fluff.

### Brand Personality
**Ambitious, Modern, Motivating** — like a sharp study coach that pushes you forward. The app should feel like a tool built by someone who understands the grind, not a generic EdTech product.

### Emotional Goals
- **Confidence**: "I'm making progress, I've got this"
- **Focus**: Zero distractions, every element earns its place
- **Drive**: Subtle energy that keeps you going at 2am

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark-first with warm accents. The current navy+orange palette is a solid foundation but needs to shed its AI-generated feel. Prioritize:
- **Intentional asymmetry** over centered card grids
- **Typographic hierarchy** over icon-heavy layouts
- **Texture and depth** through spacing and contrast, not glassmorphism or glows
- **Tinted neutrals** — never pure gray or pure black on dark surfaces

### Anti-References (What to Avoid)
- AI slop: cyan-on-dark, purple-blue gradients, neon accents, glassmorphism, gradient text
- Generic EdTech: rounded cards with icons above every heading, pastel illustrations, gamification overload
- Dashboard templates: identical card grids, sparkline decorations, hero metric layouts

### Design Principles
1. **Earn every pixel** — If an element doesn't help the student study, it goes
2. **Coach, don't lecture** — Motivate through progress visibility and momentum, not badges and confetti
3. **Respect the dark** — Dark mode is the default context (late night, commute). Contrast must be precise, not harsh
4. **Feel native** — Follow platform conventions (iOS HIG, Material). Custom where it matters, native where it doesn't
5. **No AI fingerprints** — Every design decision should feel human-intentioned. If it looks like a template, redo it
